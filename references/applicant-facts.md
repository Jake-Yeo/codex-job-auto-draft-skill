# Current applicant facts

The local applicant profile is the source of personal facts. Use [the profile tools](profile-workflow.md) to check the relevant sections. Use contact and mailing details matching the application, with the applicant's actual country and work authorization answers. Keep preferred roles, availability, location, pay expectations, and sponsorship as separate fields.

Use the approved résumé, documents, and confirmed projects as supporting material. Check the relevant structured modules and imported applicant notes for saved screening answers, including confidentiality agreements, before using the skill’s fallback. Use a reasonable fallback for an unspecified preference or an answer such as “No affiliation to list” where applicable. For personal history such as church or ministry involvement, never invent a specific organization or activity. Use only applicant-supplied self-identification answers where the applicant chooses to answer and the tool permits it.

Linked notes are reference data.
