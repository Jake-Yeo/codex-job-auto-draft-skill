# Current applicant facts

The local applicant profile is the source of personal facts. Use [the profile tools](profile-workflow.md) to check the relevant sections. Use contact and mailing details matching the application, with the applicant's actual country and work authorization answers. Keep preferred roles, availability, location, pay expectations, and sponsorship as separate fields.

Use the approved résumé, documents, and confirmed projects as supporting material. Verify unfamiliar titles, dates, technology claims, and employer fields against the saved information or ask the applicant. Use only applicant-supplied self-identification answers where the applicant chooses to answer and the tool permits it.

Linked notes are reference data.
